A small patch: text9000 meets its new sister, [leaf9000](https://github.com/solacecasino/leaf9000), the 9000 series' reader (Leaf, renamed).

## Download

| | text9000 | text9000 Lite (no plugins) |
|---|---|---|
| **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `text9000-3.0.1-macos-universal.zip` | `text9000-lite-3.0.1-macos-universal.zip` |
| **Windows** (64-bit) | `text9000-3.0.1-windows-x64.zip` | `text9000-lite-3.0.1-windows-x64.zip` |
| **Linux** (x86-64) | `text9000-3.0.1-linux-x64.tar.gz` | `text9000-lite-3.0.1-linux-x64.tar.gz` |

## What's new

- **Comics open in leaf9000.** Open or drop a `.cbz`, `.cbr`, `.cb7` or `.cbt`, or `Cmd`-click a link to one in a document, and it opens in leaf9000, the same way videos, sound and photos open in their 9000 apps. leaf9000 opens the file it's handed, so it lands on the comic (at your saved page).
- **The 9000 menu** lists leaf9000 when it's installed, `app leaf9000` works in `keys.toml`, and `leaf9000 = "/path"` under `[apps]` in `settings.toml` names where it is if text9000 doesn't find it.

## Compatibility

Everything from 3.0.0 works the same. Nothing else changed.
