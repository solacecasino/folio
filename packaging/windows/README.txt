text9000 for Windows

Double-click text9000.exe to start. Keep the themes folder next to it.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because text9000
isn't signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Your settings, themes and adapters live in %APPDATA%\text9000
(text9000 > Open Config Folder). Coming from Folio? The first start copies
everything from %APPDATA%\Folio, and .folio documents still open.
In keys.toml, "cmd" means Ctrl.

text9000 opens videos, sound and photos in its sister apps (splice9000,
wave9000, grace9000) when they're installed. Unzip them next to this
folder, or name their paths under [apps] in settings.toml.

Start here, a ten-minute guide to everything text9000 does:
https://github.com/solacecasino/text9000#readme
