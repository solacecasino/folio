Folio for Windows

Double-click folio.exe to start. Keep the themes folder next to it.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because Folio
isn't signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Your settings, themes and adapters live in %APPDATA%\Folio
(Folio > Open Config Folder). In keys.toml, "cmd" means Ctrl.
